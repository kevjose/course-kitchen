The method

Learn a rate, then scale it.

First work out how much raw produce the kitchen used for each diner each day. That makes a 20-person course comparable with a 50-person course.

Usage = opening stock + purchases
− remaining stock − spoilage

Daily rate = usage ÷ diners ÷ days

The next rate keeps 70% of the previous estimate and takes 30% from the new course. This is simple exponential smoothing.

Buy = expected use + buffer − usable stock
Then round up to the purchase increment.

Purchases alone cannot show demand. Records without an inventory check stay in the history but do not change the rate. Unknown shortages also stay out. Known unmet demand is added to usage before learning.

Use separate histories for materially different menus. This planner assumes one recurring menu.
