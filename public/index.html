    The method

    Use the evidence you have.

    Reviewed purchases establish a provisional rate. Partial receipts and nominal feeding days make that rate uncertain.

    Purchase rate = reviewed quantity
    ÷ diners ÷ recorded feeding days

    Completed courses with confirmed feeding days and inventory establish measured raw kitchen usage.

    Usage = opening + bought
    − remaining − avoidable spoilage

    The first measured course sets the rate. Later courses update it with exponential smoothing. A 260 diner-day course gets 30% weight; smaller records have less influence, with the weight capped at 51%.

    An unknown shortage retains observed usage as a lower bound and stays flagged until you record the missing demand. This minimum does not guarantee enough food.

    These choices are not fitted to your kitchen yet. Compare forecasts with future courses before trusting the buffer or scaling to a much larger kitchen.
